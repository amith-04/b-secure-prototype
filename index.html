Chapter one

From rulebook to decision, in three steps.

01

Teach it the brand once.

Admins enter approved facts, prohibited phrases, required disclaimers and their sources. Every rule has an owner, a severity and a switch.

02

Rules catch the known. AI reads the context.

Exact checks find banned wording and missing disclaimers. The AI reviews claims against your approved facts, and anything it cannot verify is labelled needs verification instead of guessed.

03

A person decides, and the record shows who.

Managers approve, request changes or reject. Approving content that still has open findings requires a written reason, and every action lands in the audit log.

Chapter three

Built to keep confidential drafts confidential.

Brand material is sensitive, so the checker is held to the same standard as the content it checks.

  • Your key stays on the server.The browser never sees the AI credential. Requests go to this site's own endpoint.
  • Only what is needed is sent.The AI receives the draft, the active rules and a strictness setting. Nothing about the visitor.
  • No trackers, no cookies, no third-party fonts.The page loads nothing from other sites. Your reading is not shared with anyone.
  • Data stays in your browser.Submissions and history live in local storage on this device. One button deletes them.
  • The endpoint does not store drafts.It forwards the request and returns the result without logging draft text.
  • Use fictional content for now.The AI provider processes drafts, so check its retention terms before using real confidential material.
